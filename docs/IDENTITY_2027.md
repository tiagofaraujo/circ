# CIRC 2027 visual identity

Implemented from organisation-supplied `Cores(2).pdf` and `CIRC27_PROPA1(2).pdf` (25 September 2026).

- Primary blue: #232EB7 (retained).
- Dark blue: #20167F.
- Accent red: #F7301D.
- Paper: #F2F1F0.
- Lilac: #CBC3E2; peach: #FBA299.
- Main typeface: Inter, locally served variable WOFF2, Latin and Latin Extended. Font licence in public/fonts/Inter-LICENSE.txt.
- Alike Angular remains available for selected existing display elements.

The bell image is the organisation-supplied `01-1000114960.jpg` (25 September 2026), used unchanged at its original 1451 × 1536 px resolution as `public/identity2027/bell-radiograph-hq.jpg`. It replaces the earlier PDF rendering. The surrounding #2F3196 background and edge gradients match the supplied JPEG.

The slogan follows the supplied `01-1000114981.jpg` reference: “The / Invisible / becomes / visible”, in four bold, left-aligned red lines inside a rounded purple panel. Its wording, capitalisation and lack of a final full stop match the poster. The panel retains the reference's 445:540 proportions and uses #432773, slightly deeper than the screenshot's purple, for at least 3:1 contrast with the event red #F7301D at the large text size. It remains a live HTML heading in both languages. Supporting copy and links sit alongside on desktop and below on mobile.

The presentation poster shows 4–5 April 2027, which conflicts with the approved website programme (courses 8 April; congress 9–10 April). The existing programme dates are retained pending clarification; the poster itself is not published with conflicting dates.
